The studio has been rearranged twice this month. This is usually a sign that something is about to change in the work.

**On the walls:** three canvases in various stages of undress, one gouache that has been pinned up since January, and a postcard of a Morandi I have stopped seeing.

**On the table:** a jar of ochre that has gone slightly waxy, two brushes I keep meaning to replace, a stone from a walk in February, and a list that says only *green — warmer?*

**Refusing to become a painting:** a photograph of a half-open door; the particular pink of a wall at dusk; a conversation I keep returning to.

Spring always makes me want to lighten the palette before the weather has actually changed. I am trying to let the paintings decide this year, rather than the calendar.

The next series will probably be about thresholds — the space of a doorway, the edge of a field, the moment before entering a room. Or it will be about something else entirely. That is the honest version of a plan.

More soon.
